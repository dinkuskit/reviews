**Who can write a review?**

Only a buyer, from the link in their review email. Each link is for one product in one order, works for 60 days and takes one review.

**Where do the emails come from?**

From the sender you pick in Settings > Email, using your store's own address. DinkusKit never sends them for you.

**Can I edit or delete a review?**

You can publish, hide or mark it as spam, and add one public reply. You cannot change a buyer's stars, words, name or date.

**Does it track opens or clicks?**

No. The emails carry no tracking pixels and no tracked links.

**What does it keep about the buyer?**

The order, the buyer's email and name, and the products bought, so it can send the request. A buyer who stops review emails is kept only as a one-way fingerprint of their address, never the address itself. Public ratings and reviews never show the order or the buyer's email.

**Where can I learn more or contribute?**

[Browse the source](https://github.com/dinkuskit/reviews) or [report an issue](https://github.com/dinkuskit/reviews/issues).
