1. Use an EmDash site with Registry sandbox support, [DinkusKit Commerce](https://plugins.emdashcms.com/plugins/@dinkuskit.com/dinkus-commerce) 0.9.0 or later, and the DinkusKit store template, which wakes the store's courier once a minute, draws the `/review` page and shows ratings on product pages.
2. Pick an email sender in **Settings > Email**. Review requests go through it.
3. Install DinkusKit Reviews and open its **Reviews** page. Review requests are on from install. Change the wait, the reminder and "approve first" there.
