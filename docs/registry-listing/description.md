DinkusKit Reviews collects star ratings and written reviews from people who bought from your store, and keeps them in your store's own database.

- **Review request email** after an order ships: one email listing every product in the order, each with its own link to your store's review page. It goes 14 days after shipping by default (you can pick 1 to 90 days). The links work for 60 days.
- **One reminder**, 7 days later by default, only for products the buyer has not reviewed yet. You can turn it off.
- **A stop link** in every request and reminder. A buyer who uses it gets no more review emails from your store.
- **The Reviews page** lists every review, newest first. Publish, hide or mark each one as spam, and add one public reply. Turn on "approve first" to hold every new review until you publish it.
- **Ratings on your storefront**: each product's average stars and review count, and its published reviews, 20 at a time.

Only buyers can review, one review per product per order. Nothing on the Reviews page can change a buyer's stars or words.
